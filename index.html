odd puzzles: two edges recede — tap the hidden point where they'd meet. even puzzles: press the bold dot and drag an edge straight into the ringed vanishing point.

scored on pure geometry: tap distance to the true point, or your stroke's angle off the true edge. six puzzles, your round score is the mean.

studio tip: every set of parallel edges shares one vanishing point on the horizon — find the horizon first and everything else snaps into place.
